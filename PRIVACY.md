# Privacy Policy — Refined PrUn – COSM Edition

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Refined PrUn – COSM Edition is an unofficial fork of Refined PrUn for the game Prosperous Universe.

## What the extension stores

- Your extension settings, notes, sorting choices and favorite contract recipients.
- This data is stored in your browser (`storage.local`). Notes and favorite recipients are also
  stored in `storage.sync`, so your browser can sync them between your own devices.
- The developer never receives this data.

## Network requests

The extension runs only on `https://apex.prosperousuniverse.com/`. Besides the game itself, it
reads public data from:

- `https://refined-prun.github.io` — public commodity exchange prices.
- `https://rest.fnar.net` (FIO) — public company data, to show player names of contract partners.

These requests do not contain personal data other than the public company code that is looked up.

## What the extension does not do

- No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
- No data is sold or shared with third parties.
- No remote code is loaded.
